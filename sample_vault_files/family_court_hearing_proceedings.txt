IN THE COURT OF THE PRINCIPAL JUDGE, FAMILY COURTS, DWARKA, DELHI
CASE NO: HMA 1120 OF 2025

IN THE MATTER OF:
Smt. Priya Sharma ... Petitioner
v/s
Shri Vikram Sharma ... Respondent

PROCEEDINGS OF HEARING - JANUARY 3, 2026
The petition under Section 13(1)(ia) of the Hindu Marriage Act, 1955, for dissolution of marriage by a decree of divorce was called for hearing.

Both parties are present in person with their respective counsels. 
Counsel for the Petitioner submitted that the parties have been living separately since March 2024 and mediation attempts have failed. 

Counsel for the Respondent requests an adjournment of four weeks to file the written statement. The Petitioner objects to any long adjournment, stating that the Respondent is intentionally delaying interim maintenance hearings.

ORDER:
1. The Respondent is directed to file the written statement and reply to the application for interim maintenance under Section 24 of the Hindu Marriage Act, 1955, within three weeks from today.
2. Advance copy of the reply shall be supplied to the Petitioner's counsel at least 3 days before the next date.
3. The matter is listed for arguments on interim maintenance and framing of issues on March 2, 2026.

Principal Judge, Family Court, Dwarka.
