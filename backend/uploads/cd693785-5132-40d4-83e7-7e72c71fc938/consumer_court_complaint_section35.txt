Smt. Anjali Roy, R/o Flat 102, Shanti Vihar, Dwarka Sector 4, New Delhi ... Complainant

MegaKart India Private Limited, Registered Office, Bengaluru, Karnataka ... Respondent

COMPLAINT UNDER SECTION 35 OF THE CONSUMER PROTECTION ACT, 2019

The Complainant states as follows:
1. The Complainant ordered a premium laptop (Model: Zenith-X1) from the Respondent's e-commerce platform on December 1, 2025, paying INR 85,000 via online banking.
2. The product was delivered on December 5, 2025. Upon opening the package, the Complainant found the screen completely shattered and the device unresponsive.
3. The Complainant immediately registered a return and refund request. The Respondent rejected the request on December 8, 2025, claiming the damage was caused post-delivery.
4. The refusal to refund for a defective and damaged product constitutes a 'deficiency in service' and 'unfair trade practice' under Section 2(11) and Section 2(47) of the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

PRAYER:
The Complainant prays that the Hon'ble District Commission direct the Respondent to:
a) Refund the complete purchase amount of INR 85,000 with 12% interest.
b) Pay INR 20,000 as compensation for mental agony and litigation costs.

Complainant: Anjali Roy
Date: January 15, 2026.
