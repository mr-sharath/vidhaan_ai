Subject: Unauthorized Use of Registered Trademark "VIDHAAN"

Dear Sir/Madam,

This notice is served on behalf of our client, M/s Vidhaan Holdings Private Limited, who is the registered proprietor of the trademark "VIDHAAN" under Class 30 (Registration No. TM-4429381 dated April 12, 2018) and Class 43 (Registration No. TM-5510293 dated October 5, 2020) of the Trade Marks Act, 1999.

It has come to our client's notice that you have recently opened a restaurant outlet using the identical name "Vidhaan Foods" and are selling packaged food items under the brand name "Vidhaan".

Your unauthorized use of our client's registered trademark constitutes:
1. Trademark Infringement under Section 29 of the Trade Marks Act, 1999.
2. Passing off your business and goods as those associated with our client.

We hereby call upon you to cease and desist from using the name "VIDHAAN" or any deceptively similar mark in connection with your business within seven (7) days of receipt of this notice, failing which our client will initiate legal action seeking injunction and damages in the High Court of Delhi.
